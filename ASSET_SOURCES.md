The editorial hair portrait (`public/images/hair-campaign.jpg`) was generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool for the homepage Beauty Inspiration section and the Hair gallery.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond and Manrope, self-hosted Latin WOFF2 variants from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font License notices are included under `public/fonts/`. The source families are available in https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope . Gujarati currently falls back to the device's Gujarati-capable fonts; full translated typography is pending review.

Flower/utility icons are Lucide React (ISC). The favicon/wordmark composition is original, provisional brand styling awaiting owner approval.
